# Gym Maturity 5x7

Five capability planes are scored from M0 Seed through M6 Enterprise. The overall gym level is the minimum plane level; documentation or a receipt alone cannot crown the gym.

Planes: Semantics; Evaluation; Runtime & safety; Evidence & replay; Operations.
Levels: M0 Seed; M1 Modeled; M2 Admitted; M3 Runnable; M4 Receipted; M5 Replayable; M6 Enterprise.
